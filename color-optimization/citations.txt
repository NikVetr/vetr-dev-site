Discriminability Threshold References

General JND scaling
- Wikipedia: "Color difference" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_difference)
  - Notes ΔE_ITP is scaled so a value of 1 indicates potential just noticeable difference (JND).
  - Notes ΔE formulas were originally designed so 1.0 is a JND, with CIE76 revised to ~2.3.
- Color.js docs: "Color difference" (https://colorjs.io/docs/color-difference/)
  - Notes ~2.3 as the commonly cited JND threshold for ΔE*ab-style metrics.

CAM02-UCS / CAM16-UCS
- Luo, M. R., Cui, G., & Li, C. (2006) “Uniform colour spaces based on CIECAM02 colour appearance model”
  (CAM02-UCS is a uniform space; Euclidean distances intended to correlate with perceived differences).
- Li, C., Li, Z., Wang, Z., Xu, Y., Luo, M. R., Cui, G., et al. (2017) “Comprehensive color solutions: CAM16, CAT16, and CAM16-UCS”
  (CAM16-UCS likewise provides perceptually uniform Euclidean distances).

OKLab scaling
- Björn Ottosson (2020) “A perceptual color space for image processing”
  https://bottosson.github.io/posts/oklab/
  (OKLab uses normalized L in [0,1] and a/b in roughly [-0.5, 0.5], so ΔE-like thresholds are scaled by 1/100 relative to CIE Lab).
