The Cooperative AI Foundation (CAIF), was founded in 2021, backed by a $15 million philanthropic commitment from Macroscopic Ventures. CAIF is a grantmaking and research organisation dedicated to supporting the growth and impact of cooperative AI, an emerging research field that strives to improve the cooperative intelligence of advanced AI for the benefit of all. We believe this field is critical because advanced AI could help address some of humanity’s most difficult cooperation problems, such as climate change or geopolitical conflict. But emerging dynamics between AI agents could also lead to serious harm.

‍

Our mission is to ensure that, as AI systems increasingly interact with each other and with people, we can understand and control the extent to which those AI systems cooperate. We also want to expand the potential of AI to improve human cooperation. You can read more about our mission here.

‍

The ‘benefit of all’ sits at the heart of everything we do. We are relentless in our ambition to have the greatest possible positive impact, both supporting and trusting our staff to perform at their best. We strive to learn from our failures, through a culture of curiosity, openness, and compassion, alongside evidence-based reasoning and rigorous evaluation. With a diversity of perspectives and values forming the core of any cooperation problem, we must also practise what we preach, maintaining plurality, equity, and inclusiveness in all that we do.

The Role
The Organisation

We are seeking a CEO with vision, ambition, and a strong link to cooperative AI, who will shape CAIF’s direction over the coming years. Since our founding in 2021, advanced AI has progressed rapidly, with much shorter timelines to the integration of advanced agents into society than previously expected. Our existing portfolio of activities, research priorities, and even our foundational understanding of what cooperative AI means may need to evolve for us to have the impact we're aiming for. Therefore, the CEO will articulate an ambitious actionable vision for cooperative AI to guide the organisation through its next phase, advancing our mission.

‍

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Strategic leadership of CAIF: working with the Board of Trustees to deliver on an ambitious vision for the next phase of the organisation.
  • Articulating a compelling theory of change and ensuring goals and activities are aligned with this. 
  • Overall responsibility for the operational effectiveness and impact of the organisation, including the use of CAIF resources to achieve agreed objectives.
  • Leading, motivating, and inspiring the CAIF team and promoting a creative impactful working environment. 
  • Managing an executive team. 
  • Representing CAIF with influential external stakeholders, key partners, and prospective partners.
  • Securing funding for CAIF’s mission and building a diversified funding base.
  • Overall responsibility for organisation governance, including compliance with UK charity law and other relevant legislation; and reporting to the Board on organisation performance, financial management and key risks.  
  • Demonstrating leadership in relation to the foundation’s values; ensuring that the culture of the organisation matches those values.

We are a fully remote organisation, with regular opportunities to meet colleagues in person (team members are currently based in the UK, Sweden, Netherlands and France). The successful candidate will work from home or from a workspace local to them.

‍

CAIF is a UK charity and we are expecting to appoint someone based in the UK to the leadership role.

Salary
Location

Salaries at CAIF account for demonstrated competencies and location-based cost of living. For this role we expect salaries offered to be between £90,000 and £140,000. 

‍

Our benefits package includes home office equipment; travel expenses to co-locate with colleagues; 33 days of paid holiday (including bank holidays); a 5% pension contribution; a generous learning and development budget; and health and wellbeing support.
