 Our Team

One for the World is run by a small team of effective giving advocates committed to building a movement of effective philanthropists. 

Based in California, Connecticut, New York, Florida, Minnesota, Washington DC, and Mexico City the One for the World team works in a fully-remote office. 

Note: 100% of your pledged contributions are directed toward our Top Picks nonprofit partners and they do not contribute to our operating costs. If you would like to support our team and donate to our operating budget, you may do so here by selecting ‘OFTW ops costs’ at stage 4 of the checkout flow.

Thank you!

Frank Fredericks - Executive Director

Frank Fredericks joined One for the World in June 2024, after his role at Accenture, where he was Senior Entrepreneur, and served as Lead for the Social Innovators Accelerator. Previously, Frank founded World Faith, a global nonprofit working to end religious violence, and Mean Communications, a digital agency for social good. Frank has written for the Huffington Post, Washington Post, and Sojourners. Frank is a graduate of NYU and received his MBA from the Saïd Business School at Oxford University as a Global Shaper Scholar. Frank lives in Queens, New York City.

Sarah Braden - Program Director

Sarah has a background in mathematics, education, and nonprofit work. Sarah is skilled in program management and expansion, as well as data-driven decision-making, and earned her Master's of International Education Policy from Harvard University. Sarah developed programming with positive outcomes for families in Dallas, TX through the United Way, and led strategy for student-facing initiatives at St. John's University in Queens, NY. Sarah taught secondary school mathematics as a Peace Corps volunteer in Mozambique, which inspired her to pursue work centered on alleviating extreme poverty globally. 

Fabiola Gil Rodríguez - Senior Program Manager

Fabiola joined One for the World in August 2025 as Operations Lead and transitioned into her current role as Senior Program Manager in June 2026. She brings 12+ years of experience in international organizations (including the United Nations), with expertise in training program design and delivery, community building, and stakeholder engagement. Fabiola holds a BA in International Relations from UNAM and an MA in Sustainable Peace from UPEACE. Originally from Costa Rica and now based in Mexico City, she is passionate about human rights, gender equality, social justice, and sharing the power of effective giving. She enjoys reading, cooking, staying active, traveling, and quality time with family and friends, and firmly believes in leading with kindness.

Liz Durán - Product Engineer

Liz joined One for the World in June 2025. She is a Computer Engineer passionate about STEM and education. She leads initiatives where technology can create meaningful community impact, designing learning programs, delivering technical training and events, and fostering inclusive tech adoption through community building. Based in Mexico City, Liz enjoys photography, dancing, and drawing mandalas in her free time.

Arturo Sánchez López - Digital Communications Manager

Arturo Sánchez is a marketing communications professional with over a decade of experience developing communication strategies across both the corporate and agency worlds. Throughout his career, he has helped organizations build meaningful relationships through storytelling, integrated marketing, and audience-first communication. At One for the World, Arturo is excited to use those same skills to help more people discover the power of effective giving. He believes the best communication doesn't just inform, it helps people make better decisions for themselves and for others, and sees joining One for the World as part of his own journey toward building a life where purpose and profession go hand in hand. Outside of work, Arturo is passionate about intentional living, personal growth, and wellbeing. He believes that becoming a better communicator starts with becoming a better human being, and that meaningful change begins with the small choices we make every day.

Varun Bandi - Chief Technology Officer (Fractional)

Varun joined One for the World in June 2025, after having worked in a variety of technical roles from engineering to managing software teams and leading technical programs and strategy at companies such as Amazon, Uber and Grubhub. He’s passionate about using technology for good, and currently is working on a financial literacy app, and volunteering for a local food bank. Outside of work, he enjoys tennis, reading, writing, and hosting cocktail and dinner parties in Brooklyn, NY.

Ana Gutiérrez Ulibarri - Operations Manager

Ana joined One for the World in July 2026. She brings over 10 years of experience in international nonprofit operations and communications, most recently at Smile Train across Latin America where she built operational frameworks, coordinated large-scale international events, and collaborated with cross-functional teams across multiple countries. Before that, Ana spent several years with Up with People managing production logistics and training coordination for global tours across 8+ countries, including creating and facilitating educational workshops centered on social justice. For Ana, meaningful work has always meant work that makes a difference in people’s lives. Based in Mexico City, she is passionate about traveling, music, films, and spending time with the people she loves.

Patricia Ramirez - Chief Marketing & Communications Officer (Fractional)

Patricia joined One for the World in March 2025. She brings experience across marketing, branding, and communications from in-house leadership roles at Fortune 500s and startups as well as on the agency side. With a passion to do good, Patricia has volunteered for United Way, Big Brothers Big Sisters, Girls Inc., and as a guest lecturer for Pepperdine University’s microenterprise program, supporting aspiring entrepreneurs from underserved communities. Patricia earned her undergraduate degree in Finance and Marketing from the University of Southern California and completed one year of law school at Chapman University. Based in Manhattan, NY, Patricia enjoys cooking, travel, and staying active with tennis, hiking, and pilates.
