Join our team

Working with governments across Africa and Asia, PxD delivers evidence-based information and tools that millions of farmers can use to improve their lives, from timely farming and weather guidance to climate-resilient inputs. Take a look at our current openings. If a role fits your skills and interests, we'd welcome your application. We offer competitive, flexible benefits to support your health and wellbeing. We evaluate candidates on merit alone, without regard to gender, gender identity, ethnicity, religion, disability, caste, or any other characteristic protected under applicable law. Personal data provided is used for recruitment purposes only.
