🐖 Animal Welfare

  • Aldi Süd commits to electrical stunning for all farmed prawns by 2035 and ending eyestalk ablation by 2030, making it the eleventh and final major UK supermarket to set a time-bound prawn welfare commitment.

  • Japanese retail giant AEON launches a comprehensive Animal Welfare Policy, marking a major milestone for cage-free progress in Japan.

  • Yurun Holdings Group, one of China’s largest meat suppliers, commits to 100% cage-free egg and chicken sourcing across all global factories by 2035.

🥗 Plant-Based Food & Alternative Protein

  • The Center for Biological Diversity launches the US Just Food Transition Roadmap, a policy framework developed to guide a shift away from industrial animal agriculture toward plant-rich food systems.

  • The Good Food Institute’s Alt Protein Project launches its first Academic Research Challenge, offering students expert mentorship, seed funding, and presentation opportunities for alternative protein research projects.

  • London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine researchers find that replacing processed meat with plant-based alternatives could make a meaningful difference across the overall diet.

⚖️ Policy, Legislation, and Law

  • Finland's updated dietary guidelines drive a shift toward plant-based proteins, with 15% of Finns changing their diets in response, and meat reduction being the most common change made.

  • US lawmakers introduce a bipartisan bill offering tax credits for plant-based biomanufacturing, aiming to boost demand for plant-based crops.

💪 Animal Advocacy Org Updates

  • Compassion in Machine Learning launches CompassionBench, a public leaderboard tracking how well frontier AI models reason about animal welfare.

  • Mercy for Animals partners with Arihanta Institute to offer discounted online courses on animal ethics, plant-based living, and food systems.

  • Project Slingshot launches as a new UK organization dedicated to exposing the myths and corporate power keeping factory farming in place, opening with a London Underground campaign targeting carbon dioxide gassing of pigs.

📚 Research

  • Animal Justice, Humane World for Animals, Mercy for Animals, and the Montreal SPCA release a report outlining critical gaps in Canada’s current approach to farmed animal welfare.

  • Animal Think Tank finds that focusing on animals’ emotional experiences is more effective at building public empathy than comparing speciesism to racism or sexism.

  • Faunalytics explores the data on corporate outreach for cage-free eggs and the Better Chicken Commitment, examining what drives companies to act, how pledges are secured, and what influences successful follow-through.

  • Researcher Rakefet Cohen Ben-Arye surveys Chinese vegan advocates, finding that health rather than animal welfare is the primary driver of plant-based diets.

  • Researchers find that framing animal welfare information in relative rather than absolute terms leads to higher purchase intentions for higher-welfare poultry products.
