Special Projects

Rethink Priorities' Special Projects Program accelerates impactful initiatives by providing comprehensive support services, enabling organizations to focus on their core mission and scale efficiently.

The Special Projects Program offers fiscal sponsorship, finance administration, recruitment, and operational support to new and growing initiatives. Our aim is to help high-impact projects get off the ground quickly, providing the infrastructure needed to scale efficiently.

Let’s work together.

Join Special Projects Program

We’ve helped several initiatives already and are currently accepting expressions of interest for new projects.

From handling tax compliance to event planning, we help ensure your project is set up for success.

Fees for new projects are typically around 14% and vary depending on the needs, complexity, and scale of each project. We will be happy to share more details with you as we come to understand your project’s specific needs.

Our services

The SP Team provides each initiative that it supports with operational assistance to ensure day-to-day functioning. This support enables the initiative’s staff to focus on their core mission and activities and get the project off the ground faster than they likely would have otherwise.

Receiving tax-exempt grant funds on your behalf

Handling tax and legal compliance issues

Accounting

Hiring employees via our US or UK legal entities or internationally via our Employer of Record in compliance with local laws, which allows us to hire people in many countries

Managing employee benefits and payroll

Invoicing and contracting, as well as handling purchasing and reimbursements

Helping to manage project budgets

Facilitating staff applying for work visas in the US or UK

Researching relevant legal and operational issues

Developing hiring and interview materials

Running your hiring rounds

Coordinating and reviewing grant applications written by project leaders

Managing donations made by individuals

Planning the logistics for online or in-person events

Assisting projects in developing agendas and other materials for events

Sourcing a venue, accommodations, catering, AV, and other suppliers

Developing event policies (e.g. COVID policy)

Guest RSVPs and registration

Training and upskilling managers and supporting goal-setting, performance management, and performance review processes

Writing newsletters, website copy, and other communications materials

Creating business plans for projects

Helping to run competitions or requests for proposals

Providing ad hoc operational support
