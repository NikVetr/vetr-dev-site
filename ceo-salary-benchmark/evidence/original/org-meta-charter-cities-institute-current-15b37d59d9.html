Our Mission

The Charter Cities Institute is a nonprofit dedicated to creating the ecosystem for charter cities, founded on the idea that a fresh approach was necessary to tackle humanity’s most pressing challenges: global poverty, rapid urbanization, and climate change. We believe charter cities—new cities granted a special jurisdiction to create a new governance system—are that solution.

 

Here at CCI, we help coordinate and accelerate the development of charter cities through research & technical assistance, partnerships & events, and media. Join our team of academics, economists, entrepreneurs, futurists, humanists, technologists, urbanists, and experimental thinkers focused on the development of charter cities!
