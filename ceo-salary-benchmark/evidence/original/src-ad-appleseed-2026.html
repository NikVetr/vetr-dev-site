Human Rights & Justice Full-time

Executive Director

Appleseed Foundation

Posted

Jun 29, 2026

Location

Remote (US)

Type

Full-time

Compensation

$120000 - $155000

Mission

What you will drive

  • Lead the development and execution of strategic priorities for the Appleseed Foundation, ensuring alignment with the needs of justice centers and communities.
  • Build strong relationships across the network, foster collaboration, and coordinate multi-center initiatives for systemic change.
  • Serve as principal ambassador and spokesperson, driving fundraising and external partnerships to support the network's sustainability.
  • Oversee organizational operations, staff leadership, and board relations to ensure effective governance and financial health.

Impact

The difference you'll make

This role advances social justice by stewarding a binational network of justice centers that pursue systemic solutions through policy advocacy, impact litigation, and community partnerships, directly benefiting marginalized communities.

Profile

What makes you a great fit

  • Significant senior leadership experience in nonprofit organizations, with demonstrated success in fundraising and donor relationship management.
  • Strong understanding of systemic social justice advocacy, including policy advocacy, impact litigation, and community partnerships.
  • Experience working with networks, coalitions, or decentralized collaborative models.
  • Exceptional interpersonal and communication skills; J.D. or other relevant advanced degree preferred.

Benefits

What's in it for you

Salary range: $120,000-$155,000, commensurate with experience. Competitive Benefits Package. Fully remote position within the United States.

About

Inside Appleseed Foundation

Visit site →

The Appleseed Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that serves as the hub for the Appleseed Network, a community-driven network of 20 justice centers across the US and Mexico advancing social justice through policy advocacy, impact litigation, and research.
