Careers

Join us as we build bold solutions for a new America.

At New America, our values guide our work and our commitment to meaningful impact. Supporting our people is central to that commitment. We offer a comprehensive rewards package designed to foster well-being and growth, including competitive, market-aligned compensation; health and wellness benefits; generous paid time off; and leave programs. All benefits are subject to eligibility requirements detailed in our policies.

If purpose-driven work excites you and you want to help shape what’s next, explore our current openings and join us.

Open Call for Contractors

Interested in contributing to New America’s mission through project-based consulting work?

New America periodically partners with independent consultants and contractors who bring specialized expertise, project-based support, and additional capacity to our programs and operational teams. These opportunities are paid project-based consulting opportunities, not employment positions, and may vary based on organizational needs.

To express interest in future consulting opportunities with New America, please complete this form. As project needs arise, your information may be reviewed by New America staff across different departments. You will only be contacted if your background, expertise, availability, and services appear to align with a specific project need.
