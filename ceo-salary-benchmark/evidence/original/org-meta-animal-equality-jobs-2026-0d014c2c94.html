WORK WITH US

Our international team in eight countries is made up of talented individuals with a wide range of backgrounds who work passionately to save animals.

Animal Equality is committed to professional development, workplace learning, and career progression. People with diverse backgrounds are encouraged to apply*. Learn more about what it’s like to work at Animal Equality.

OPENINGS IN THE UNITED STATES

Undercover Investigator (full-time, remote, exempt)

Undercover Investigator UK (contract, remote)

Undercover Investigator Germany (contract, remote)

Salesforce Administrator & Developer (full-time, remote, exempt)

Social Media Strategist (full-time, remote, exempt)

If you are looking for more opportunities, consider volunteering with us! Joining our team of Animal Protectors is a great way to learn about the impact of our work first-hand by participating in actions and roles that make a difference for animals.

* Note: We can only consider applicants who possess United States work authorization.
When you apply for a job at Animal Equality, we collect personal data from you during the evaluation process. Read our Prospective Hire Privacy Notice for more information.
