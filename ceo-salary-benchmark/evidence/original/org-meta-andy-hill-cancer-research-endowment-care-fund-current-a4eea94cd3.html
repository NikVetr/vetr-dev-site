Employee Benefits

CARE Fund is a Washington-based, fully remote organization committed to communication, collaboration, innovation, adaptability, teamwork, and a healthy work-life balance. Washington State residency is a requirement for employment with CARE Fund.

  • Competitive pay
  • 100% employer-paid medical/dental/vision insurance for employees and eligible dependents
  • A 401(k) retirement plan with a non-elective employer contribution
  • Vacation starting at 12 days per year in the first year of employment
  • 12 days of sick leave per year
  • 8 floating holidays per year
  • Thanksgiving and the day before Thanksgiving and the week between Christmas and New Year’s as scheduled holidays
  • A flexible, 4-day work week
