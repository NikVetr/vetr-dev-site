Employment

PIIE is a private, nonprofit, nonpartisan research institution devoted to the study of international economic policy. Since 1981, the Institute has provided objective analysis and concrete solutions to a wide range of international economic problems. The Institute sponsors events and produces scholarly studies in a wide range of formats.

If you are interested in joining our accomplished staff, please look through any employment opportunities that may be below.

Please note we do not offer internships.

PIIE is an equal opportunity employer.
