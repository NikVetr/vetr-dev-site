Adam Shifriss

Adam Shifriss is the Senior Director of Legislative Strategy for the Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget, guiding education and engagement with congressional offices in both parties to advance responsible budget policies. His work has contributed to shaping major legislation and debate in areas including budget process reform, debt limit, tax, health care, and Social Security. He helped establish and grow the member-led Bipartisan Fiscal Forum. Adam previously served in the U.S. House with a portfolio including Energy and Commerce and Appropriations Committee work. He holds bachelor's and master's degrees in Policy Analysis from Indiana University. Contact him at shifriss@crfb.org.
