Working at Wild Animal Initiative

We're known for our positive work environment.

Each year since 2020, Animal Charity Evaluators has recognized us for our high standards of organizational health and employee satisfaction. We offer professional development opportunities, a comprehensive benefits package, generous paid time off, and more.

Benefits of working at Wild Animal Initiative

Career growth

  • Opportunities to shape your role and responsibilities as our organization evolves.

  • Networking and speaking engagements at scientific conferences, workshops, and seminars.

  • Reimbursement for books that support professional development.

Health insurance

  • Comprehensive employer-paid health and dental insurance. Coverage for spouses and dependents is available, with WAI covering a portion of that cost.

  • Vision insurance plans also available.

  • Additional benefits that support employee health, including online mental health therapy through Talkspace. 

Remote but connected

  • Fully remote, flexible work environment that allows you to choose when and where you work.

  • Access to remote work tools that make communication easy. 

  • Friendly, open work culture that encourages feedback, collaboration, experimentation, and evidence-based innovation.

  • Regular team retreats where we invest in bringing our remote team together safely with evidence-based health protocols.

Support & training

  • Leadership and colleagues dedicated to justice, equity, diversity, and inclusion, including an internal JEDI committee. 

  • Annual staff and manager training to combat our biases at work.

  • Deep commitment to supporting employees with visible and invisible disabilities.

Generous paid time off

  • Four weeks of paid vacation.

  • Two weeks of paid vacation at the end of December.

  • 13 additional holidays.

  • Two weeks of paid sick leave, which may also be used to care for human or non-human family members.

  • Up to six weeks of discretionary leave for circumstances such as caring for a newborn child, adopting a child, dealing with a health condition that leaves them unable to work, caring for a family member with a serious health condition, or bereavement. 

  • Additionally, we offer seven weeks that are an exclusive benefit for new parents, as well as an additional 12 unpaid weeks, for a total of 26 weeks possible parental leave.

Compensation

  • We set our salaries using an algorithm that takes into account the local cost of living where our staff work. We do this to ensure equity within our team and to make sure our staff’s basic needs are met with a little left over.*

  • Employer retirement contributions: WAI contributes to retirement savings for all staff. In the US, this is a 401(k) match. For staff in other countries, WAI makes employer contributions to your national retirement system.

*Note: We use this benefit structure for US staff and offer a similar structure to many non-US staff. However, these calculations may look different depending on the legal requirements of the country where you work.
