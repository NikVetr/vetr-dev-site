Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Alliance to Feed the Earth in Disasters (ALLFED) is an effective altruism aligned organization that is an alliance of three nonprofit entities with charity status (in the US, UK, and Australia) and has team members in over a dozen countries. Its mission is to build resilience to global catastrophic food system failure. ALLFED currently operates under a collective directorate model, with four directors/heads of department sharing executive leadership responsibilities rather than a single CEO.

We value diversity of experience, perspective, and background, and consider them essential to our mission. We welcome applications from individuals of all genders, races, ethnicities, sexual orientations, ages, abilities, religions, nationalities, and socioeconomic backgrounds.

Summary: 

The CEO will take the overall charge of ALLFED and: 

  • Work with the Board to support good governance practices and to maintain compliance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, including IRS 501(c)(3) regulations

  • Oversee and work closely with all members of the executive management team.

  • Lead interactions with other organizations and key partners.

  • Lead development and implementation of ALLFED’s strategy, plans, and programs.

  • Take responsibility for financial sustainability.

  • Safeguard relationships between ALLFED’s sister entities (ALLFED’s Alliance).

Reports To:

Board of Directors.   

Duration and Hours:

Part-time flexible hours, but will need to coordinate with multiple time zones. Suitable for candidates with disabilities, caring responsibilities or other pre-existing commitments.

Location:

Remote/home working. Some in-person meetings, depending on location. 

Remuneration:

USD 60 - 65 /hr – To be agreed based on scope and experience.

Key Responsibilities:

GOVERNANCE:

  • Ensures that all required filings (IRS Form 990, registrations, audits, etc.) are completed accurately and on time.

  • Oversees recruitment of Board Members and Trustees.

  • Works with the Board to support maintenance and further development of good governance practices.

  • Implements policies set by the Board and ensures decisions are executed effectively.

  • Provides the Board with the information and context it needs for sound decision-making.

  • Safeguards the organization’s reputation and public trust, including during crises.

  • Ensures strong internal controls, necessary audits, and financial transparency.

LEADERSHIP:

  • Contributes and leads on strategy for the organization as a whole.

  • Participates in thought leadership — writing, speaking, and contributing to sectoral debates.

  • Ensures ongoing monitoring of ALLFED’s impact as well as development and implementation of suitable impact indicator metrics.

  • Oversees all programs and ensures the organization meets appropriate charitable metrics (such as the program-to-overhead ratio). 

  • Improves the external impact of ALLFED as an organization, research outputs, and influence on government agencies, working with ALLFED’s alliance and other impact indicators.

  • Builds the profile not only of our charity itself but also of the issues we seek to advance.

  • Anticipates and responds to external opportunities and risks (e.g., policy, funding, sector trends).

  • Represents the organization publicly as a chief spokesperson (often alongside active co-founder David Denkenberger or other internal spokespeople).

  • Ensures the timely delivery of organizational commitments, reports, and OKRs.

  • Facilitates top-level organizational prioritization.

  • Models ethical leadership and accountability.

  • Fosters an inclusive, diverse, ethical, and mission-driven organizational culture.

TEAM:

  • Recruits, develops, manages, and inspires ALLFED’s executive management team.

  • Ensures cohesive coordination between ALLFED’s internal departments to facilitate alignment with the core organizational strategy for achieving the mission.

  • Oversees operations, internal systems, HR policies, and performance management across the team

FUNDRAISING:

  • Serves as a primary relationship builder for key donors, funders, and partners inside and outside the Effective Altruism community.

  • Works closely with the Legal Board to set annual revenue targets, diversify income streams, and ensure long-term financial sustainability.

  • Meets directly with major donors, foundations, and institutional funders to cultivate relationships and secure large gifts.

OTHER TASKS AS REQUIRED

Essential (must have):

FOR ALL ALLFED ROLES:

  • A mature, open-minded attitude - we are trying to help feed the world in case of a global catastrophic food failure!

  • Awareness of ALLFED’s work and mission.

  • Self-motivated, reliable, hard-working, and able to get along with people.

  • Comfortable with working remotely and using online collaboration systems. 

  • Has access to appropriate equipment and technology (high-speed Internet, computer, headset + webcam).

  • Excellent spoken and written English.

ROLE-SPECIFIC

  • Desire to accomplish ALLFED’s mission of building resilience to global catastrophic food system failure which could deplete 5% or more of global calorie output, or access to those calories.

  • Ability to prioritize, organize, and delegate tasks effectively in alignment with the organizational mission and goals.

  • Skills to safeguard ALLFED’s mission by ensuring all activities contribute meaningfully to a better world, remain strategically aligned, and are delivered cost-effectively.

  • Demonstrated success in steering organizations through uncertainty or transformation, fostering resilience, and aligning teams around renewed strategic priorities.

  • Experience building strategic partnerships and facilitating successful collaborations with governments, industry, academia, and other nonprofits.

  • Ability to cultivate an overall organizational environment that allows team members to achieve their goals.

  • Experience recruiting, coordinating, and leading teams.

  • Motivation to empower team members to find their comparative advantage and do their best work.

  • Strong financial literacy and ability to interpret budgets, identify cost-saving opportunities, and make strategic trade-offs under constrained resources.

  • Commitment to maximizing impact per dollar spent and ensuring transparent, responsible use of funds.

  • Excellent project management skills and knowledge of relevant tools and methodologies.

  • Ability to identify and manage organizational, financial, and reputational risks.

  • Excellent analytical and reporting skills.

  • Excellent networking, presentation, and persuasion skills.

  • Demonstrates openness to feedback, learning, and iterative improvement as the organization adapts to new realities.

DESIRABLE (NICE TO HAVE)

  • A degree in management, communications, or public relations.

  • Experience leading complex fundraising initiatives and cultivating major funding relationships to support organizational growth and mission delivery.

  • Established networks within philanthropy, including high-net-worth individuals, institutional donors, and foundations.

  • Experience engaging with policy and decision-making circles at national or international level.

  • Experience in cause/charity field-building.

  • Past history of leading an effective research or impact-driven charitable organization.

  • Experience working in global emergency response and humanitarian crises situations.

  • Knowledge of the global food production and distribution system.

  • Experience in leading fully remote global teams across a wide range of time zones.

  • Communications background, including brand management and/or media handling.

  • Policy or technical background, including research experience.

  • Professional memberships.

  • Familiarity with Global Catastrophic Risks.

  • Familiarity with Effective Altruism and its core principles.

  • Multilingual and culturally competent.

  • Scout mindset.
