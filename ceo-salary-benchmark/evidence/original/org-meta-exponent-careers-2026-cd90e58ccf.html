Careers

Exponent Philanthropy is a vibrant membership association that provides resources and connections to help funders make the most of the minutes they have and the dollars they give.

Please read the instructions below for our members submitting openings in the field for our careers page.

Working At Exponent Philanthropy

Exponent Philanthropy is the largest philanthropic network in the country and the only membership association that specifically focuses on foundations, families, companies, and individuals who practice philanthropy with lean operations.  With nearly 1,500 members representing every corner of the U.S., we are a nationwide network of community-connected philanthropy leaders. Our vibrant membership network represents all 50 states that embrace a style of philanthropy motivated by personal passion, community need, and a strong desire for better outcomes.

We seek bright, highly collaborative, ethical, intellectually curious colleagues who are enthusiastic about connecting and serving our members. Our staff members are passionate about our mission and core values and believe strongly in the power of philanthropy to transform communities.

Open Position

Thank you for your interest in the CIR position! We are no longer accepting applications.

 

Catalytic Philanthropy – Changemaker in Residence (1099 or P/T)

The Changemaker in Residence (CIR) is an experienced philanthropic leader and Catalytic Philanthropy practitioner who will help scale Exponent Philanthropy’s signature Catalytic Philanthropy initiative from a successful pilot into a nationally recognized field-building movement.

Embedded within Exponent Philanthropy’s team, the CIR will:

  • Expand and professionalize Catalytic Academy’s program portfolio, reach, and impact as a high-impact national model for community-centered philanthropy
  • Design, launch, and train a Catalytic consulting and coaching service model supporting coaches, guides, and staff for long-term sustainability of the program
  • Collaborate and cultivate philanthropic investment in partnership with the Advancement team
  • Serve as a Catalytic Philanthropy ambassador and provide field-wide thought leadership that extends Catalytic Philanthropy beyond Exponent’s membership as a movement for the broader sector

This role brings lived experience, credibility, and entrepreneurial thinking to accelerate CLIP’s reach, sustainability, and impact across the philanthropic sector. An experienced lean funder/donor (CEO, staff, board member, etc.) is preferred.

Compensation:
Reports to the Managing Director of Knowledge. 1099 (preferred) or part-time W-2. 15–30 hours/week. $4,000 – $10,000/month, depending on hours; retainer or hourly. Additional travel budget available. Compensation to be revisited if the role becomes full-time.

We actively welcome candidates from communities that have been historically excluded from philanthropy, including people of color, people with disabilities, LGBTQ+ people, veterans and others who bring perspectives often missing from our field. We are an equal opportunity employer and consider all qualified applicants.

    Submitting Job Openings

    This job board is for openings in philanthropy organizations, philanthropy support organizations, and for specific opportunities (within other organizations) that are associated with services we believe to be of interest to our members. We reserve the right to determine what specific positions are appropriate for posting.

    Exponent Philanthropy members may submit a website link or a document (Word or PDF) to info@exponentphilanthropy.org.

    Non-member philanthropy organizations may submit a website link to info@exponentphilanthropy.org. We do not post openings from search firms (we can’t keep up with the number of these requests).

    Postings will be active for 60 days.
