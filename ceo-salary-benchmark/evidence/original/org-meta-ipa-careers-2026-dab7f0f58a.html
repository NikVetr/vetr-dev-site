BE CAUTIOUS ABOUT EMAILED JOB POSTINGS. Innovations for Poverty Action (IPA) will never charge a fee at any stage of the recruitment process nor request sensitive personal information over the phone or by email. All recruitment correspondence you may receive will be from a 'poverty-action.org' email address.

Why Work For IPA?

Our people and partners.

Our passionate, dedicated, and intellectually curious staff work alongside top minds and leaders in international development. Get to know the colleagues, researchers, and partners who help us drive positive change.

Read More

We are committed to making a positive impact.

Our organization is driven by its mission: we discover and advance what works to improve the lives of people living in poverty. Learn more about what we do and our impact.

Read More

Leadership development & career growth.

Our staff are handed serious responsibility and gain career-defining experience. We invest in staff development through global staff training events and other staff learning opportunities.

It’s rewarding.

Our benefits package provides a positive work-life balance. View our current opportunities page to see examples of job roles, responsibilities, and locations for IPA staff.

Read More

We support fully remote and hybrid work options.

We want our staff to have a sustainable work culture, whether in one of our offices, at home, or both. View our current opportunities page to see open positions at IPA across the globe.

We work across a diversity of sectors.

Our sectoral expertise includes agriculture, climate and environment, consumer protection, education, entrepreneurship and private sector development, financial inclusion, human trafficking, health and nutrition, intimate partner violence, peace and recovery, and social protection.

Our Values

Commitment to Improving Lives

We exist to improve the lives of people living in poverty through evidence and we care deeply about achieving our mission.

Rigor

We are unrelenting and rigorous in our pursuit of effective solutions and the use of data.

Integrity

We hold ourselves and our partners to the highest ethical standards in all we do and how we do it.

Co-Creation

We work to co-create relevant evidence and locally-rooted change, always guided by the context of the countries in which we operate.

Diversity

Our diverse ideas, experiences, and commitment to treating all people with dignity and respect allow us to make a greater impact locally and globally.

Collaboration

We build strong relationships, based upon mutual respect, with researchers, partners, and each other to achieve greater impact.
