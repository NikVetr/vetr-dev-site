      About Us

      FAR.AI is a non-profit AI research institute working to ensure advanced AI is safe and beneficial for everyone. Our mission is to facilitate breakthrough AI safety research, advance global understanding of AI risks and solutions, and foster a coordinated global response.

      We’re structured to support that work from early research through real-world adoption:

      Independent by design. We can pursue what's most impactful based on our theory of change and share what we find publicly.

      A portfolio approach. Rather than focus on one single direction, we run diverse bets across the safety stack. We take promising ideas from initial experiments to deployment, informed by red-team partnerships with frontier labs and governments.

      Serious infrastructure for ambitious research. A dedicated engineering team runs our compute cluster and experiment-scaling stack, so researchers spend their time on research instead of on infra.

      Setting the standard. Our events convene key decision makers; our red-team works with frontier developers and governments; and our communications inform the public. Together, this drives adoption and sets the new standard in safety.

      Since our founding in July 2022, we've grown to 50+ staff, published 40+ academic papers, and convened leading AI safety events. Our work is recognized globally, with publications at premier venues such as NeurIPS, ICML including a Best Paper Honorable Mention in 2026, and ICLR, and features in the Financial Times, Nature News, Wired Magazine and MIT Technology Review. We conduct pre-deployment testing on behalf of frontier developers such as OpenAI and independent evaluations for governments including the EU AI Office and publish the AI Security Leaderboard based on our red-teaming expertise. We help steer and grow the AI safety field through developing research roadmaps with renowned researchers such as Yoshua Bengio; running FAR.Labs, an AI safety-focused co-working space in Berkeley housing 40+ members; and supporting the community through targeted grants to technical researchers.

      The Role

      We are hiring a Business Operations Associate or Senior Business Operations Associate to support our Business Operations Manager and work closely with our COO and leaders across the organization. This is a hands-on generalist operations role with a broad remit: you will own the compliance tracker, provide finance and accounting support, assist with HR operations, assist with travel and events, support FAR.Labs, help with IT coordination, take on special projects as they arise, and fill gaps where there's no clear owner.

      You will report to our Business Operations Manager and collaborate closely with our COO and the rest of the BizOps team. Your priorities will shift as organizational needs evolve. No two weeks will look the same, and that is by design.

      While both roles share the same core remit and require a successful track record of delivering critical improvements in intra- and inter-team operations, a Senior Business Operations Associate would bring deep experience in owning and building operational systems and processes in startup-like environments, exhibit nuanced thinking in driving improvements across teams, and take full ownership of a portfolio of internal systems.

      Why This Role / Why Now

      FAR.AI is scaling fast. We are 45 people today and expect to grow to 75+ by the end of the year. Our operations team is growing alongside the organization, and the scope of what we need to run well is expanding: finance and accounting, HR and compliance, IT, FAR.Labs, and a steady stream of special projects that do not fit neatly anywhere else.

      This role owns the recurring work that keeps FAR.AI moving. Partnering with our Business Operations Manager, you’ll be the dependable center of our operations, making sure nothing slips and who teams trust to just handle it. You’ll own work that’s varied, hands-on, and close to every part of the organization. If you do excellent work and the fit is strong, there is real room to grow with us. The operations function is being built, not maintained, and this is an opportunity to be part of that.

      If you are sharp, low-ego, and energized by getting things done across a wide range of operational domains, we would love to hear from you.

      Key Responsibilities

      • Operations delivery: Own operational tickets, triaging incoming requests and providing first-responder support across the team. Own and improve our operational requests pipeline, maintain policies, and support recurring operational rhythms. Drive operational excellence and clear any roadblocks that might slow us down.
      • Systems & continuous improvement: Support the design, maintenance, and optimization of FAR.AI’s cross-team coordination systems, operational tool stack, and knowledge management systems. Spot operational risks and process gaps early, and propose solutions. Identify and execute on opportunities for process automation and systems integration as FAR.AI scales.
      • Finance & compliance: Own and maintain FAR.AI's compliance calendar, tracking federal, state, and local filing deadlines and ensuring nothing falls through the cracks. Support the systematization and automation of compliance obligations across the organization, support grantmaking operations, and provide financial operations support as needed.
      • IT coordination: Provide initial IT support and coordinate with vendors to keep the team's tools and systems running. Coordinate equipment provisioning and sourcing.
      • HR operations: Support onboarding and offboarding processes, HR compliance, employment policies, and the maintenance of the company handbook.
      • Coworking space operations: Support the operations of FAR.Labs, including event logistics.
      • Special projects: Take on ad hoc operations projects end-to-end and drive specific pieces of larger initiatives.

      Must-Have Qualifications

      • Professional experience in operations or program/project management, with demonstrated success building and implementing operational systems and processes. Consulting backgrounds are welcome, but please note that we'll want to see evidence of real operational execution and iteration.
      • Demonstrated experience as a high-agency problem-solver. You've repeatedly identified a need or a better way to do something and brought the solution to life, without being asked.
      • Evidence of real results in past roles: things you have delivered or problems you have solved while juggling several responsibilities at once.
      • Comfort working across multiple operational domains simultaneously: finance, HR, compliance, IT, and projects. Even if you’re not a domain expert, you've ramped up in new areas quickly before and can learn fast.
      • Superb written and verbal communication. You can adjust your communication for different audiences, summarize a situation accurately, and write clear, concise documentation.
      • Reliable with details, especially on compliance and finance tasks, where a missed deadline or a wrong number is a real issue. You flag issues and develop solutions proactively.
      • Comfort zooming between the details and the big picture. You can take an ambiguous problem, ask the right questions to scope it, and chart a path to a solution, without losing track of the specifics that make or break execution.
      • Demonstrated service mindset. You get energy from helping others succeed – no job is too big or too small.

      Nice-to-Have Qualifications

      • Experience in fast-paced, highly ambiguous environments. You've worked in startups, nimble nonprofits, and/or high-growth teams before.
      • Experience contributing to an impact-driven organization, or a passion for AI safety.
      • Experience, and driving change within an organization.
      • Experience driving change within an organization, with a sense for creating the right amount of structure to enable, rather than stifle, a team that’s scaling fast.
      • Experience with tools like Coda, Notion, Asana, CRMs, or similar operational software.
      • Fluency with AI tools and workflows, paired with the judgment to know when to use them and when not to.
      • Experience building automations for efficiency gains.

      Logistics

      • Structure: You will be a full-time employee of FAR.AI, a 501(c)(3) research non-profit.
      • Location: Berkeley, CA (onsite/hybrid) OR Remote (U.S)
      • Hours: Full-time (40 hours/week).
      • Compensation: $90,000-$110,000/year for the Business Operations Associate role, or $110,000-$125,000/year for the Senior Business Operations Associate role, depending on experience. We will also pay for work-related travel and equipment expenses, and offer catered lunch and dinner at our office in Berkeley.
      • Reporting: Reports to the Business Operations Manager.
      • Hiring process: A test task, phone screen, interview with the hiring manager, and in-depth interviews with the team, followed by reference checks.
