# Third Expansion Protocol Amendment

**Original benchmark cutoff:** 2026-08-17  
**Third-extension freeze timestamp:** `2026-08-25T17:12:00-0700`  
**Prior frozen candidate universe:** 305 organizations  
**New third-extension candidates:** 145 organizations  
**Combined three-wave universe:** 450 organizations

## Purpose

Further extend the publicly sourced candidate universe and re-perform the RP CEO compensation benchmark. This amendment preserves the original protocol and both earlier expansion files unchanged. It does not retroactively convert previously observed compensation into blind evidence.

## Independence and exposure

All organizations marked `new_pay_eligible=yes` were named and frozen before systematic review of their compensation in this third wave. Measures for Justice was discovered through a compensation-bearing page before this freeze; it is marked `context_only_no_primary_use` and may not affect primary estimates, tier thresholds, weights, or recommendations. The prior-wave exposure logs remain controlling for earlier organizations.

No organization may be selected, promoted, demoted, weighted, or removed because its compensation is high or low. Post-freeze exclusions are limited to role/title structure, interim/co-CEO/partial-year status, legal-entity mismatch, scale, operating model, geography, filing recency, and source adequacy.

## Selection and evidence rules

The second-expansion scale, title, geography, evidence, normalization, and sensitivity rules remain unchanged. Tier A generally requires a clean full-time organization-wide CEO/President-CEO/ED and close functional plus operating-scale fit. Tier B permits one meaningful scale or operating-model deviation. Tier C is broad sensitivity only. Fiscal sponsors, grantmakers, university centers, affiliates, co-leadership structures, and very large or small entities remain separately labeled.

For every compensation observation, use no more than one most-recent full filing available by 2026-08-17; identify the actual compensation calendar year; keep Part VII reportable compensation, related-organization pay, other compensation, Schedule J base, bonus, deferred compensation, and total compensation distinct; and never describe Part VII reportable compensation as exact base salary. Current job advertisements remain a separate forward-looking evidence stream.

## Completion targets

The combined three-wave universe exceeds 400 organizations. The quantitative target remains at least 100 usable incumbent observations and at least 15 current recruitment advertisements. Missing targets must be disclosed rather than achieved by relaxing title or transition rules after viewing pay.
