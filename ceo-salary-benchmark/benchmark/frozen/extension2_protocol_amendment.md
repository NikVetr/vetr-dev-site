# Second Expansion and Rebenchmark Protocol Amendment

**Original benchmark cutoff:** 2026-08-17  
**Second-extension freeze timestamp:** `2026-08-25T16:07:55-0700`  
**Purpose:** further expand the peer universe and re-perform the CEO compensation benchmark using the expanded set while preserving all earlier frozen files and results as visible baselines.

## Independence and exposure

This phase is not blind to the prior benchmark or to compensation already collected for the original nine peers. The analyst has also encountered incidental compensation for several organizations in the first 160-name expansion while verifying public scale. Those exposures are preserved and cannot influence tiering, weights, or exclusions.

The new organizations in `extension2_new_candidates_precomp.csv` were named and frozen before systematic review of their executive compensation. Compensation later encountered during scale or structure verification cannot alter selection except for pre-registered structural reasons: title, interim/co-CEO/partial-year status, legal-entity mismatch, scale, operating model, geography, or source adequacy.

## Reference-set construction

The prior 160-name universe remains intact. The second extension adds US and US-relevant nonprofits in AI and emerging technology, security and biosecurity, general policy and evidence research, philanthropy infrastructure, open science, climate, global health and development, and animal welfare/food systems.

## Scale and role rules

RP's primary core anchor remains approximately $7.5M-$9.3M of core operating scale and roughly 45-57 staff. Candidate scoring uses function, operating expenses/budget, headcount, EA affinity, clean organization-wide CEO structure, and US national labor-market relevance. Consolidated pass-through, grants, fiscal-sponsorship, and affiliate flows are not treated mechanically as core operating scale.

- **Tier A:** strong functional match; clean solo organization-wide CEO/President-CEO/ED; generally $4M-$30M expenses and 20-125 staff, with limited rule-based exceptions.
- **Tier B:** useful functional or EA-adjacent peer with one meaningful scale, geography, or operating-model deviation; generally $2M-$60M and 8-200 staff.
- **Tier C:** broad sensitivity only, including larger/smaller organizations, delivery-heavy organizations, or less direct functional matches with otherwise usable CEO observations.
- **Structural/context:** university centers, fiscal hosts/projects, private foundations, grantmaking/pass-through-dominated entities, international-only labor markets, affiliates, co-CEOs, interim or transition cases, and unclear organization-wide leadership.

No organization may be selected, promoted, demoted, or removed because its compensation is high or low.

## Compensation evidence

1. Use the most recent full Form 990 available by the August 17, 2026 cutoff. A filing first filed after the cutoff is ineligible.
2. Prefer official IRS XML object identifiers and canonical IRS URLs. ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer may be used for navigation and corroboration.
3. Use no more than one observation per organization.
4. Primary observations require a full-time, organization-wide CEO, President/CEO, top-executive President, or Executive Director.
5. Exclude interim, co-CEO, regional/subsidiary, partial-year/transition, and unclear top-executive cases from primary summaries; retain labeled sensitivities.
6. Keep Schedule J base, Part VII reportable compensation, related-organization compensation, other compensation, bonuses, deferred compensation, and total compensation distinct.
7. Part VII reportable compensation is a cash/W-2 proxy, not exact base salary.
8. Do not restrict the sample to organizations with Schedule J.

## Recruitment evidence

Current 2025-2026 CEO/President-CEO/organization-wide ED advertisements are a separate forward-looking stream. Do not pool posted base ranges with incumbent filing compensation.

## Normalization and analysis

Use CPI-U, U.S. city average, all items, not seasonally adjusted, series CUUR0000SA0, targeting July 2026. Determine the compensation calendar year under IRS reporting conventions.

Report unweighted and comparability-weighted medians, quartiles, ranges, trimmed/winsorized sensitivity, and sample sizes for Tier A; Tier A+B; Tier A+B+C; EA-core/EA-adjacent versus functional-only; close versus wider scale; title versus functional matches; geography; founder/unusual structure exclusions; job ads versus filings; and leave-one/category-out tests. Do not describe the sample as a population percentile.

- Clean incumbent compensation observations: target at least 75; aspirationally 100.
- Current recruitment advertisements: target at least 15 usable observations.

If a target is missed, report it rather than relaxing structural or title rules after viewing pay.
