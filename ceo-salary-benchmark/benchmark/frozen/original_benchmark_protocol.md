# RP CEO Independent Compensation Benchmark Protocol

**Protocol status:** FROZEN before viewing any peer executive compensation or peer job-ad salary ranges  
**Frozen timestamp (America/Los_Angeles):** 2026-08-17T17:52:30-0700  
**Target date:** 2026-08-17  
**Scope:** Full-time, organization-wide Chief Executive Officer of Rethink Priorities only.

## Independence and contamination log

No internal RP salary document, workbook, memo, board paper, proposed CEO salary, prior report, prior peer list, or other analyst conclusion was supplied or used. During Phase 0 scale research, a public search result for RP's 2024 Form 990 unexpectedly displayed RP officer-compensation figures before this protocol was frozen. Those target-organization figures are quarantined: they will not be used to choose peers, define methods, choose percentiles, calibrate ranges, or validate conclusions. This protocol is therefore pre-peer-compensation but not fully pre-target-compensation. Any later comparison to RP's own pay, if mentioned at all, will be descriptive and post hoc, outside the benchmark estimate.

## Public RP role profile frozen for scale matching

Primary scale anchor: 2026 core operating budget of approximately USD 7.5M, with approximately USD 9.3M identified as productively usable; current public roster count of 45 unique named core staff (including Special Projects team) and a historical 2024 anchor of 57 FTE / 61 permanent employees. Consolidated 2024 expenses of approximately USD 20.4M include material fiscal-sponsorship/pass-through activity; they will be a sensitivity, not the mechanical primary scale measure.

## Functional peer criteria

Primary peers must be independent nonprofit organizations whose dominant executive challenge is knowledge production and organization-building: research, policy analysis, evidence synthesis/evaluation, decision support, strategic consulting/advisory work, research infrastructure, or a close hybrid. They should operate nationally or internationally and require the top executive to integrate research direction/quality, strategy, fundraising, board relations, talent/culture, and operations.

Cause-area familiarity alone is insufficient. Peers dominated by direct field delivery, mass-membership services, university administration, hospital/education operations, government, commercial consulting, or pure grantmaking/regranting are excluded from the strict primary tier.

## Numerical organization-scale criteria

Strict primary scale band, evaluated using the most decision-relevant operating measure available:

- Core annual operating expense or budget: USD 5M-20M.
- Core staff: approximately 25-100 FTE.
- At least one of the two numerical criteria must be inside the band and the other no more than 50% outside it.
- When consolidated filings include sponsored projects, pass-through grants, or regranting, use a documented core-program measure where available; retain consolidated scale as a sensitivity.

Secondary tier may include organizations with USD 3M-35M core operating scale or approximately 15-150 staff when functional similarity is unusually strong, or organizations inside the primary size band with a material structural complication (founder dominance, umbrella fiscal sponsor, advocacy-heavy model, or substantial grantmaking/pass-through).

Organizations above USD 35M or below USD 3M core scale are excluded from the main analysis unless used in a clearly labeled scale-band sensitivity.

## Geographic and labor-market criteria

Primary geography is the US national market for remote or multi-state knowledge-sector nonprofits, with major-hub competition from Washington, DC, New York, Boston, and the San Francisco Bay Area. Internationally distributed organizations with substantial US hiring or a comparable UK/global research labor market may enter the secondary tier. Local-only organizations in low-cost labor markets are excluded from the strict primary tier unless they recruit nationally/remote.

No city cost-of-labor multiplier will be imposed mechanically. Geography sensitivity will compare US-remote/national postings, high-cost-hub postings, and UK/global postings separately.

## Relevant titles

Eligible primary titles: Chief Executive Officer; President and CEO; Executive Director when the role is the full-time organization-wide top executive with board accountability and broad strategy/fundraising/operations responsibility. President alone is eligible only when clearly the top executive. Chief Scientist, Research Director, Managing Director, Country Director, Regional Director, subsidiary CEO, program CEO, project director, and other subordinate roles are excluded.

## Recency cutoffs

- Recruitment advertisements: primary window 2025-01-01 through 2026-08-17; secondary sensitivity 2023-01-01 through 2024-12-31.
- Form 990: most recent full filing publicly available by 2026-08-17, normally tax year 2024 or 2023. Older observations are used only if no newer complete filing exists and are labeled.
- Compensation calendar year will be determined from filing instructions and Schedule J/Part VII context, not assumed from fiscal-year end.

## Evidence tiers

1. **Primary recruitment evidence:** complete employer or retained-recruiter posting with an explicit annual base-salary range and enough role/scale information to assess comparability.
2. **Secondary recruitment evidence:** complete source-native job-board copy or complete lawful mirror when the original is inaccessible; older ads are separately labeled.
3. **Primary incumbent evidence:** official IRS e-file XML or source-native filing PDF, with complete return retained where technically obtainable.
4. **Secondary incumbent evidence:** ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer rendering/API or another filing-derived source used for navigation/corroboration; canonical IRS object/URL recorded where available.
5. **Survey evidence:** only credible nonprofit compensation surveys with explicit organization-size, role, geography, year, and measure definitions. Generic salary websites are excluded from quantitative synthesis.

Search-result snippets are discovery aids only and are not evidence observations.

## Inclusion and exclusion rules

- Maximum one primary observation per organization.
- Include only full-time organization-wide top executives with substantially comparable responsibilities.
- Exclude regional/country managing directors, government officials, interim executives, subsidiary leaders, project/sponsee leaders, and subordinate executives from primary analysis.
- Co-CEOs are excluded from primary analysis and may appear only in a labeled sensitivity.
- Partial-year incumbents are excluded from primary analysis; no silent annualization. A separately labeled annualized sensitivity may be shown only when dates and pay period are clear.
- Founders are allowed in secondary analysis but excluded in a founder sensitivity; founder status is not itself a reason to discard a strict functional/scale match.
- Organizations dominated by direct field delivery are excluded from primary even if size matches.
- Organizations dominated by grantmaking, regranting, pass-through funding, or fiscal sponsorship use a core operating scale when reliably separable; otherwise they are secondary or excluded.
- Interim roles, acting roles, and vacant-role caretakers are excluded.
- If exact title/role status cannot be verified, observation is excluded and logged.
- No observation may be removed because it appears unusually high or low. Structural outliers remain in the data and are addressed through pre-registered sensitivities.

## Recruitment-ad collection fields

Organization, role title, posting/closing dates, base-salary minimum/maximum, confirmation that range is base salary, location/remote restrictions, mission/model, budget/expenses, staff count, responsibilities, reporting relationship, original and resolved URL, retrieval timestamp, archive path, byte length/hash where obtainable, evidence provenance, tier, and exclusion reason.

## Form 990 collection fields

Organization, EIN, IRS object ID, tax period, filing/retrieval date, exact title, full-year status, Part VII reportable compensation from organization and related organizations, Part VII other compensation, Schedule J base, bonus/incentive, other, deferred, nontaxable, and total compensation, revenue, expenses, grants, employee count, transition/founder/co-CEO/related-organization complications, and exact XML/PDF locations.

Schedule J base is treated as exact base only when disclosed. Part VII reportable compensation is labeled a cash/W-2 proxy, not exact base. The sample will not be restricted to Schedule J filers.

## Inflation normalization

Target dollars: July 2026 USD, using BLS CPI-U, US city average, all items, not seasonally adjusted (CUUR0000SA0), the most recent published index available by 2026-08-17.

- Calendar-year incumbent compensation: annual-average CPI-U for the actual compensation calendar year divided into the July 2026 target index.
- Fiscal-year filings: determine actual calendar-year compensation convention from the filing; do not assume fiscal-year-end year automatically.
- Job ads: posting-month CPI-U to July 2026 when month is known; otherwise annual-average CPI-U for posting year, clearly flagged.
- UK amounts, if used, are analyzed separately in native currency unless a contemporaneous, documented FX conversion is necessary; they are not silently pooled with US dollars.
- Every factor and source index will be reported.

## Measures kept distinct

- **Advertised base salary:** stated annual fixed salary range only.
- **Schedule J base compensation:** base salary as separately disclosed.
- **Cash/reportable proxy:** Part VII reportable compensation from organization plus related organizations; may include bonus and taxable items.
- **Other compensation:** Part VII column F or Schedule J nontaxable/deferred/other components.
- **Total compensation:** Schedule J total when available; otherwise Part VII reportable plus other compensation as a clearly labeled filing total proxy.

No uniform benefits percentage will be used to back-solve base salary.

## Statistical summaries

Recruitment ads: full envelope; exact intersection of ranges (or explicit statement that none exists); median of posted midpoints; median minimum and maximum; simple unweighted summaries; functional-match versus title-match views.

Filings: n, minimum, 25th percentile, median, 75th percentile, maximum separately for Schedule J base, cash/reportable proxy, and total compensation. Quantiles use linear interpolation equivalent to R type 7 / NumPy default. Small samples will be described as samples, not market percentiles.

## Pre-registered sensitivities

1. Strict primary peers only.
2. Primary plus secondary peers.
3. Job ads only.
4. Form 990 only.
5. Functionally matched versus title-matched roles.
6. Core scale USD 5M-20M versus wider USD 3M-35M.
7. Leave-one-organization-out medians and ranges.
8. Exclude founders.
9. Exclude partial-year, interim, co-CEO, and transition observations.
10. Exclude unusually large organizations above USD 20M core scale.
11. Exclude unusual compensation structures or related-organization pay.
12. US remote/national versus high-cost-hub versus UK/global geography.
13. Core operating scale versus consolidated scale.
14. Schedule J base-only versus broader cash-proxy evidence.

## Interpretation rules

External recruitment will be interpreted primarily from current advertised base ranges, with a recommended posting range broad enough to recruit nationally and a market center based on the midpoint distribution. Incumbent pay will be interpreted from filing-based base/cash/total evidence, recognizing lag and survivor/selection effects. No exact percentile or dollar-to-the-dollar recommendation will be claimed.
