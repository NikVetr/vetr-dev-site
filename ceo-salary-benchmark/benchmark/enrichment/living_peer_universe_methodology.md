# Living peer-universe review

## Purpose

The historical frozen peer files remain immutable audit checkpoints. They are not permanent eligibility boundaries. `living_peer_universe_review.csv` is the dated, row-level amendment that applies the same admission rule to the original cohort and every post-freeze compensation observation recovered through 2026-08-30.

The review separates whether an observation is valid from how similar its organization is to Rethink Priorities. Compensation magnitude is never used to decide status, tier, or weight.

## Default admission rule

A CEO observation enters the living default only when all of the following hold:

1. The legal entity and executive employer are resolved.
2. A preserved public filing or equivalent source directly reports a positive point amount for a named executive.
3. The person is a full-time, solo, organization-wide chief executive. CEO, President/CEO, top-executive President, and Executive Director titles can qualify when the source and governance context establish that scope.
4. The amount represents an eligible full compensation period without interim, transition, affiliate, subsidiary, or co-leadership ambiguity.
5. Non-pay evidence supports an A, B, or C peer classification.

Founder status, small or large scale, a combined board-officer title, operating-model differences, and the use of Form 990-EZ are retained as peer metadata. They do not invalidate an otherwise clean observation. Part-time, transitional, co-leader, and unresolved affiliate observations remain sensitivity or observed-only records. Explicit zero remains a validated zero but cannot enter positive/log distributions; aggregates, bands, payroll, and absent disclosure are not converted into point salaries.

## Tier and weighting treatment

The living review does not silently impose a second fixed set of weights. The initial app view remains equal-weighted so its empirical meaning is transparent. Its existing optional controls then express similarity without changing observation validity:

- incumbent Auto-weights use only filing-comparable expenses and employee count relative to RP, including the documented missing-staff penalty;
- recruitment Auto-weights use the frozen non-pay match score;
- Tier A/B/C, structure, area, EA relationship, evidence stream, title, and recency multipliers remain explicit user choices; and
- no salary value enters any automatic or category weight.

Post-freeze comparability scores remain `provisional` where the supplied package did not reproduce their component calculations. They are displayed as analyst metadata but are not used by the incumbent Auto-weights model.

## 2026-08-30 amendment

Six post-freeze organizations with valid full-time solo leadership observations enter the living default:

- Center for Election Science — Tier C;
- Foresight Institute — Tier B;
- Leverage Research — Tier C, structure flagged;
- Magnify Mentoring — Tier C, Form 990-EZ and missing-staff limitations;
- GiveWell — Tier B, grantmaking/scale flagged; and
- Copenhagen Consensus Center — Tier C, founder/scale flagged.

Qualia Research Institute remains observed-only because the filing reports 15 hours per week. The two LEEP co-executive-director rows remain separate, organization-balanced sensitivity observations. IWPR and CLASP remain outside the default because their recovered amounts are partial-year or transitional. Animal Equality and Compassion in World Farming USA remain structural sensitivities because the organization-wide employer boundary is not clean. Seattle Botanic Gardens remains a recruitment sensitivity because its posting describes a planned public-agency/nonprofit integration.

Center for AI Safety's corrected filing and Project Healthy Children/Sanku's entity-deduplicated current filing remain default observations. Thirty-three other researched candidates do not have an accepted positive, named, entity-resolved CEO point observation. They remain in the enrichment research inventory and cannot enter a salary distribution unless future source evidence resolves that gap.

## Count reconciliation

The amendment changes the CEO incumbent default from 116 to 122 positive rows. Four of the six promoted rows report Schedule J base, so the default Schedule J sample changes from 110 to 114. With the existing 15 validated recruitment postings, the initial all-evidence base view contains 129 observations representing 126 unique organizations. Leverage Research and Magnify Mentoring appear in cash and total views but not in the base view because no Schedule J base is reported or inferred.

The source archive, selected-reference inventory, incumbent observation inventory, and visible quantitative sample are different denominators. Archived pages can support discovery, classification, exclusion, or multiple observations; they must not be described as salary observations.
