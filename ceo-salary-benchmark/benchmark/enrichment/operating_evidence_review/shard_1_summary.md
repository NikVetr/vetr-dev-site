# Shard 1 operating-evidence review

All 101 organizations received a targeted current employer-careers/ATS search and a separate historical or archived-job search attempt on 2026-09-05. Each JSONL record preserves the actual queries and outcomes. Classifications remain `unknown` where followed material did not establish a work arrangement; a city label alone was not treated as an attendance requirement.

The evidence basis is 39 explicit statements, 15 mixed-role sets, 9 multiple-ad inferences, and 38 insufficient-evidence outcomes. Confidence is high for 44 records, medium for 22, and low for 35.

Hiring scope is `us_only` for 37 organizations, `international` for 23, and `unknown` for 41. Operating scope is US for 51, international for 43, outside-US for 1, and unknown for 6. These fields are separate: operating scope reflects documented footprint, while hiring scope reflects actual role eligibility where available.

CEO hiring geography remains intentionally sparse: 93 `unknown`, 6 `us_only`, and 2 `international`. Three values have direct CEO/ED role evidence (`ceo_scope_basis: direct_role`); the remaining 98 have `unknown` basis rather than deriving executive labor markets from organizational footprint.

Evidence records retain `published_date` separately from `retrieved_at`, correctly identify the publisher/source type, and point to downloaded source captures only when the local file exists. Several employer pages returned access blocks on direct retrieval; those records retain the followed URL and the access limitation rather than claiming a saved capture.
