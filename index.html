Welcome!

This website is very much a WiP. Over the years, I've made lots of little tools and utilities for myself, only to consign them to the dustbin of history once whatever immediate need has been met. But later on it's sometimes difficult to find them again, and even more difficult for me to share them. To help with both issues, I'm going to try tossing them on here, bandwidth permitting.
