# Example sources

## alice-drink-me.png

Alice with the "DRINK ME" bottle, John Tenniel's 1865 illustration for *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*, in the coloured version of *The Nursery "Alice"* (1890).

- File: [Alice drink me transparent.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alice_drink_me_transparent.png) on Wikimedia Commons (491 × 900 px), whose background was removed by the Commons user Matankic from [Alice drink me.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alice_drink_me.jpg).
- Licence: public domain. Commons tags it `{{PD-1923}}` (published before 1923), and Tenniel died in 1914. Removing the background adds no new copyright.
- Preparation: scaled to 60 % (295 × 540 px) with `convert "Alice_drink_me_transparent.png" -resize 60% -strip alice-drink-me.png` (ImageMagick 6.9), so the first render is quick.

Chapter I, "Down the Rabbit-Hole", of Lewis Carroll's *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* (1865), without its title, as in the R version's `input/txt/alice_chapter-1.txt`. The text is in the public domain.
