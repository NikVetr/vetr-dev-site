# Form / Motion trainer

The figure and clothing were adapted from the supplied Form / Motion studio. Its anatomical mesh, body-shape targets, rig, weights and fitted clothing derive from MakeHuman and MPFB assets distributed under [CC0 1.0](CC0-1.0.txt).

- [MakeHuman hm08 body](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj)
- [MPFB body-shape targets](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/tree/master/src/mpfb/data/targets/macrodetails) and [standard rigs](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/tree/master/src/mpfb/data/rigs/standard)
- [MakeHuman system clothing](https://github.com/angavrilov/makehuman-assets) and [asset pack](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html)
