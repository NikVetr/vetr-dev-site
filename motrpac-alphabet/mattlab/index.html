Painting & navigation

Click a piece
Paint with the active brush. Repeated clicks cycle the linked row’s shades, then transparent blank, then back to the first shade.

Scroll over artwork
Cycle Paint, Erase and Pick tools. Scroll backward to reverse.
Middle-click
Change the brush color family.
Right-click
Move the hovered piece’s color family, or the hovered palette row, to the next unused row throughout the study, keeping shade columns and color groups separate. Hold Shift to move to the previous row.
Drag a row marker
Move all its colors in the current design to another row. Or tap the marker, then the destination row’s name.
Erase / Background
Erase reveals the background. Choose its color beside Solid/Checker; edges turn white on dark backgrounds. Opaque exports use the chosen color.
