    How to read the rankings

    Shift is the change from the catalog expectation after entering your ratings. Select an item to inspect its full predicted distribution. The cutoff ranking counts all probability at or above your chosen rating. Mean / variance divides the expected rating by its conditional variance, floored at 0.01 squared rating units.

    The historical reference treats ratings as points on a logit scale. The experimental ordinal model treats them as intervals and updates the probability of each taste component. Its predictions integrate over latent ratings. Both models report expectations on the displayed rating scale.
