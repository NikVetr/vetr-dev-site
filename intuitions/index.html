Statistical intuitions

Small interactive figures, each isolating one statistical idea. Move a few parameters and watch the figure respond. Everything is computed exactly in your browser, by the same code a script can call through the API.

The geometry of correlation

Every variable is an arrow and every correlation the cosine of an angle. Rotate the arrows in 3-D to see why a correlation matrix must be positive semidefinite, which correlations can go together, how far apart k variables can be, and how much room one entry has.

Pooled or stratified outlier removal

A z-score cutoff on pooled groups removes the wrong share of values and takes them unevenly from the groups. See how much, for any gap in means, ratio of spreads, group share and cutoff.

The threshold model

Discrete traits from a hidden normal liability. Drag thresholds, let two correlated traits fill a contingency table, and fit a table back to its polychoric correlation.
