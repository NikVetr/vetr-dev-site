        Rating and ranking

        Rate entries in the catalog or use Quick rate with the keyboard. Choose Filters to narrow the recommendations. With Auto-update off, choose Update predictions to apply your edits.

        Familiarity weight sets the order in Most informative to rate and Quick rate. Choose 0% for the most informative questions or 100% for entries you are most likely to recognize. Each score estimates how much one answer would narrow down your taste class, in bits. A fractional bit is common. Similarity-based predictions can also change within a class. Compare validation scores to see whether ratings predict a different entry for a held-out person.
